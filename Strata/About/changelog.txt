[h1]Strata - 4.2.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Storms reach underground (with Stormproof)[/b] — rain, thunderstorms and ion storms make seep floods bigger without a running sump pump; the seismograph shows surface weather and storm surge.
[*] [b]Deep Survey[/b] — a powered seismograph can survey the rock under your deepest level and lock what the next dig will find (lake, lost rooms, magma, ore, gas).
[*] [b]Order colonists on other floors[/b] — keep selection when you change floors; drafted Go-here walks the stairs. Setting: "Select and order across levels".
[*] [b]Parties across floors[/b] — colonists on other floors join parties and gatherings. Setting: "Cross-level gatherings".
[*] [b]Fuel from another floor[/b] — refuellers fetch fuel from a linked floor. Setting: "Fetch fuel from other levels".
[*] [b]One-way stairs[/b] — each stair/elevator end can block colony traffic one way without sealing air or heat.
[/list]
