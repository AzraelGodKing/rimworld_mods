[h1]Homesteader — 1.0.3[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Pantry tab[/b] — preserved kinds in cellars, icehouses, springhouses, shelves, crates, and barrels; days of food; what's closest to spoiling.
[*] [b]Spoilage triage[/b] — colonists eat the most-spoiled stack first, and preserving bills take produce closest to turning (Mod Options, on by default).
[*] [b]Preserve crate[/b] — pack a mixed lot of pantry goods into one tradeable crate at the jam cauldron or canning kitchen.
[*] [b]Saved seed[/b] — harvest barley, pumpkin, herbs, sugar beet, and flax drops seed from your own stock. Replant it and the line slowly picks up yield, frost, or dry-spell grit. No extra UI.
[*] [b]Update letter[/b] — loading a colony shows this version's notes from About/changelog.txt, with a link to the newest full notes.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Optional art pack[/b] — unique refresh sprites are no longer in the Workshop download. Original art is what you get. Drop [i]Textures/HomesteaderRefresh[/i] from the GitHub repo into the mod folder to enable Use refreshed textures.
[*] [b]Settings[/b] — if that folder is missing, the toggle is hidden and originals stay on.
[/list]
