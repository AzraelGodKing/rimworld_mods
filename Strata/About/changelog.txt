4.3.0
[h1]Strata - 4.3.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Performance timing[/b] — Mod options > Strata > Diagnostics has a "Start performance timing" button. It times every Strata tick, update and patch while it runs, and when you stop it writes the slowest ones to Player.log and Logs/StrataPerf.log. It costs nothing until you start it. Handy for bug reports about lag.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Faster on RimWorld 1.7[/b] — the reservation checks, see-below drawing and cross-floor job checks read game data directly instead of through reflection, and gas clouds use one lookup instead of two.
[/list]

[b]Fixed (4.2.3)[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Haulers no longer carry the same items up and down the stairs[/b] — shortfall trips carry only what the other floor is missing, and items carried to another floor are not auto-hauled back for a few hours (prioritised hauls still can).
[/list]
