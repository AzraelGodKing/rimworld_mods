2.0.0
[h1]NanoHive Reforged — 2.0.0[/h1]
One package for RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Saves from 1.x keep working.
[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Buttons on every hive[/b] — repair buildings, items, gear and inventory on or off, radius (1 to 30) and repair rate (1 to 10 HP per target). Each hive saves its own settings. "Use as colony default" sets what new hives start with, and "Copy to all hives" copies one hive to the rest of the map.
[*] [b]Repair budget[/b] — each repair cycle a hive spends at most 40 targets' worth of repair (rate x 40 HP), most damaged first. With the default rate, a hive with 40 or fewer damaged things in range repairs exactly as before. "Buildings first" spends the budget on buildings before items and gear.
[*] [b]Idle power[/b] — a hive draws 200 W while nothing in range needs repair, and its full 1600 W only while repairing.
[*] [b]Only stored items[/b] — optional: repair only items in a stockpile or storage that are not forbidden.
[*] [b]Repair shimmer[/b] — a soft pulsing glow on a hive while it repairs, and a spark that fades on each thing it fixed. Turn it off in the mod settings. It only changes what you see.
[*] [b]Inspect pane[/b] — shows the hive's radius, rate and budget, how many things in range are damaged, and how much it repaired last cycle (or that it is idle or unpowered).
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b] — soft support. Hive settings live in the save and every hive button is synced, so players with different mod settings stay in step. No Multiplayer DLL is needed.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Mod settings are now starting values[/b] — they set up a new colony, or a 1.x save the first time it loads (so its hives keep working exactly as before). After that the save keeps its own values.
[*] [b]Radius ring[/b] — the selection ring and the placement ghost now show the hive's real repair radius. Before, they always showed 12 even when the radius was changed.
[*] Repaired buildings now leave your colonists' repair list as soon as they are full, so nobody walks over to fix a wall the hive already fixed.
[*] [b]Stuff[/b] — the hive is now built from metal only (wood and stone made a fireproof nanite port flammable). Hives already built from wood or stone keep working.
[*] Faster repair cycle: the cells in range are worked out once per hive instead of every cycle, and the cycle no longer allocates memory. It shows up as "NanoHiveReforged.TickRare" in the profiler.
[*] The settings window no longer writes the settings file every frame while it is open.
[*] [b]Built for 1.7[/b] — the 1.7 DLL is built against the 1.7 game on .NET 10. The 1.6 DLL is still built for 1.6.
[*] Player.log prints the NanoHive version and build on startup, and the Azrael series hub shows it.
[/list]
