[h1]Strata — 3.6.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Core sampling drill[/b] — after a few hours, reports ore, gas, infestation pressure, and rock hardness for the floor below that cell as a range. Research narrows the band. Sample more than one hole; the stratum is not uniform.
[*] [b]Deep listening post[/b] — hears movement through rock (direction and size, never a cell). Quiet floors hear farther; mining and industry on this floor deafen it. Sealed stairs still carry sound.
[*] [b]Water table[/b] — optional (off by default). Temperate tiles sit at about B2; B3+ seeps (with Unlimited). Surface and B1 stay dry. A powered sump holds only its radius; stairs and the Levels tab show the table.
[*] [b]Noise attracts the dark[/b] — optional (off by default). Loud floors raise infestation weight.
[/list]
