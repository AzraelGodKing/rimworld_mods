NEMESIS

Someone remembers. Someone is coming back.

RimWorld 1.6 Requires Harmony C# mod Personal antagonist
Steam Workshop ⬇ Download zip View on GitHub

Overview

A named pawn from a hostile faction becomes a personal antagonist — not another faceless raid. They harass your colony with taunts, false leads, waste drops, power sabotage, and personal assaults that flee when the fight turns. The hunt ends when you capture or kill them… or when they get what they came for.

Credit: original design and core runtime by Dredd (Misakabob). Expanded and packaged here by AzraelGodKing with gratitude.
Needs: Harmony. Load Nemesis after it. Safe to add mid-save; resolve any active hunt before removing.

How it starts

Trigger chances are configurable in mod settings.

Killed ally or kin

A colonist kills someone’s ally or kin. Someone takes it personally.

Prison break / slave escape

A prisoner (or Ideology slave) breaks out — and a former friend of the colony does not forgive it.

Wounded escape

An enemy is wounded, escapes the map, then comes back for the colonist who cut them.

Fixation

An enemy who killed one of yours fixates on a surviving colonist and makes it personal.

What they do

Harassment escalates over time. Settings control aggression pacing and the action mix.

Taunts & notes

Comms + paper

Comms taunts and paper notes that name your people. Soft-compat lines can reference Homesteader cellars / favorites and Stormproof ion flavor when those mods are loaded.

Raids & vengeance army

After escapes

Faction raids — and after escapes, vengeance army returns that inject the same nemesis into a heavier assault. Why return alone when you can return with an army?

Personal assault

On-map assaults that pull back when losing. They heal between escapes so the hunt keeps coming.

False signals

Fake distress / trader offers that become delayed ambushes.

Sabotage & waste

EMP / grid sabotage, Biotech waste drops, caravan harassment, and food-store vandalism.

Anomaly bait

Optional Anomaly baiting when that DLC is active (fail-open without it).

How it ends

Capture

Arrest them alive, then Execute / Release / Keep / Truce from the resolution dialog.

Corner & kill

After enough escapes they can finally be finished for good.

They win

If they hunt a specific colonist, the hunt also ends if that pawn dies or is handed over — they get what they came for.

Soft compat

Optional and fail-open — Nemesis never hard-requires sibling mods.

  • Stormproof — EMP sabotage respects EMP dampeners; surge protectors blunt grid sabotage.
  • Strata — prefers surface home maps; aware of underground / upper levels when choosing targets.
  • Homesteader — food raids and taunts can reference root cellars and favorite foods.
