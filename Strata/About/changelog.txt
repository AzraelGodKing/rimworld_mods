3.18.0
[h1]Strata - 3.18.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Drafted orders across several floors[/b] — when a drafted pawn cannot walk to the spot you clicked, it can now go down, cross more than one linked floor, and come back up a stair that reaches it. Before, only a single floor in between was tried.
[*] [b]Lost floors[/b] — rarely, a newly dug level from B2 down hides sealed rooms cut long ago by people who dug down before you. Break in to find their remains, their loot, and what killed them: insects still nest in the far rooms. Their diaries tell the story, and the lesson: noise underground draws infestations. Part of "Special levels" in the Levels settings.
[*] [b]Flooded levels[/b] — a newly dug level from B2 down sometimes holds an underground lake in the rock. Once your miners break into it, the water seeps out across the level unless a powered sump pump is running somewhere on that level, and it spreads faster while it rains hard on the surface. Anyone lying in the flood water, downed or asleep, can drown. With Odyssey you can fish the lake, and with Homesteader a well on the level above draws twice the water. Turn it off with "Special levels" in the Levels settings.
[*] [b]The magma layer, the bottom of the world[/b] — dig one level past the usual limit (B3, after the new deep bore research) and you reach the magma layer. Nothing can be dug below it. The rock sits at 52°C, so colonists need cooling down there. Magma pools light up the dark and set fire to anything flammable left beside them. Build a geothermal tap on a magma vent for 6000 W of free power, and a lot of heat and steam. Mine the obsidian seams beside the pools for obsidian, a tough and beautiful building stone. With Unlimited levels on, choose how deep the magma layer is (B8 by default), or turn it off in the Levels settings.
[*] [b]Pawns take the shorter way between floors[/b] — floors and stairwells are now picked by how far a pawn actually walks on the floors in between (walls, doors and slow ground included), not by how many stairwells it passes. A stair that cannot be walked to from where the pawn lands is not used at all. With two shafts down, they use the one whose stairs line up instead of a closer stair that means crossing a whole level. Elevators count as a quicker ride than stairs.
[*] [b]Settings presets, tabs and search[/b] — Mod options now open on tabs (Levels, Commuting, Atmosphere, Threats, Performance, Visuals, Advanced, Diagnostics) with a search box that finds any option by name. Performance, Balanced and Immersive presets set the costly options in one click; change one yourself and it shows Custom.
[*] [b]Modder API v2[/b] — other mods can ask which level a map is, its name, how many stairs away another level is, register their own work for the work relay, and hear when a level is created, removed or linked. Docs: https://github.com/AzraelGodKing/rimworld_mods/blob/main/Strata/API.md
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Colonists no longer stand idle after a work trip[/b] — a pawn sent to another floor for one kind of work refused every other job while that work was blocked (no materials, out of reach, taken by someone else), for up to half a day. They now do whatever local work they can, and give the trip up after about an hour.
[*] [b]Fewer trips up and down the stairs[/b] — a floor a pawn just left empty-handed stays off their list until it actually gains work they do. Before, any change there (another colonist finishing a job, a stack being moved) sent them straight back.
[*] [b]No endless detours[/b] — when a job is only reachable by going around through another floor and the detour still can't reach it (for example walls going up on the upper floor), the pawn stops trying that detour for a while.
[*] [b]Turning the work relay off now takes effect at once[/b] — pawns already assigned to another floor, including ones from your save, are let go.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] Automated tests now check that cave layouts, deep-level oxygen and the "leave for better work" choice stay the same between versions. No gameplay change.
[/list]
