[h1]Nemesis — 1.1.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Tells[/b] — each hunt rolls a voice, weapon family, mark, and habit that stay the same until the hunt ends. Gear upgrades quality, not the weapon type.
[*] [b]Dossier[/b] — a colony tab for name, faction, focus, tells, sightings, taunts, last-known tile, and aggression.
[*] [b]Informants[/b] — pay silver at a comms console (or the dossier) for a lead. Sometimes it is real. Sometimes they sold you a lie.
[*] [b]Bounty[/b] — put silver on their head. It is recorded; other factions may or may not care.
[*] [b]Epitaph[/b] — when a hunt ends, the dossier keeps who they were and how it finished. If Deep Colony is loaded, a copy lands on the Legacy tab.
[*] [b]Compat IDs[/b] — extra Rimesis / Back for Vengeance packageId candidates, plus a Player.log line when one is actually loaded.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] Calling-card graphic no longer points at Steel as a single texture (that path is a stack folder). It uses the vanilla component stack sprite so the def loads.
[/list]
