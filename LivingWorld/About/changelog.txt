1.2.0
[h1]Living World - 1.2.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Inter-settlement caravans[/b] - NPC caravans travel between friendly settlements on the world map. Arrivals go in the chronicle without a letter. Rarely, one passing near your colony turns aside to trade. Toggle in Mod Options.
[*] [b]World state view[/b] - a button on the Chronicle tab shows current wars, tensions, and alliances between NPC factions.
[*] [b]Severity filter[/b] - the Chronicle tab can show all news, normal and major, or major only.
[*] [b]War scar timer[/b] - war-site inspect text says how long until the site fades.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Refugees and warbands[/b] no longer fire early with a random faction. They wait for the intended delay and always come from the war you heard about.
[*] [b]Rumour corrections[/b] still arrive when the original news has aged out of the chronicle.
[*] Debug "Force" morph actions ignore the per-settlement cooldown, as intended.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] Settlement label and inspect lookups are constant-time on large worlds.
[/list]
