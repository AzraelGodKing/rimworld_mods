3.12.1
[h1]Strata - 3.12.1[/h1]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Gas no longer vanishes when the atmosphere system hits errors[/b] — the error guard used to switch both gas options off, which emptied the gas in every room on every map, and re-arm could not switch them back on after a restart. A tripped system now just pauses until you re-arm it in Mod options > Diagnostics or restart. Your options are never changed.
[*] [b]Faster hauling between levels in big bases[/b] — with a higher-priority stockpile on another level, every hauler rescanned every stored item on the map. Strata now builds that list once every few seconds per level.
[*] [b]Barns, prisons and mech crews below ground stay awake[/b] — levels with tame animals, mechs or prisoners but no colonists were treated as empty, so their temperature and haul lists ran slowly.
[*] [b]Switching floors no longer skips a gas update[/b] — changing the viewed level in the middle of an atmosphere cycle could skip gas effects on pawns or run gas sources twice.
[*] [b]Caged canaries from older saves stay in their cage[/b].
[*] [b]Compact roof decks ignore natural rock roof[/b] — only built roofs set the deck size.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] Small speed-ups for carts, loading docks and packed haulways.
[/list]
