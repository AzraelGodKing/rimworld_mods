STRATA

V2 — dig down, build up, breathe deep

RimWorld 1.6 Requires Harmony Odyssey optional C# mod
⬇ Download Strata V3 Roadmap View on GitHub

Overview

Carve a basement under your colony, frame a roof deck above it, and treat every floor as one home. Colonists commute for work, food, rest, hauling, and rituals. Power, smoke, and (optionally) pipes follow the shafts with them.

Needs: Harmony. Load Strata after it. Odyssey unlocks gravship stairwells only — everything else works without the DLC.

What’s in V2

Short version of the whole release.

▼

Dig down

Research digging down, build an excavated stairwell, open a full-size rock level under your map. Deeper floors need deep excavation. Second shafts join the same level.

▲

Build up

Research building up, frame a tower stairwell, open outdoor Level +N as a roof deck — walk and build where the floor below is roofed. Expand roofs downstairs to grow the deck.

☁

Atmosphere

Smoke, deep gas, O₂, and CO₂ share one sim. Vent, pipe, or exchange through shafts. Deep gas poisons and can ignite; oxygen pumps and scrubbers keep sealed rooms livable. Gas overlay in play settings.

⚒

Living below

Shoring, gas airlocks, ore hoists, fungus farms, sump pumps, mine lamps, bellows, lime scrubbers — plus a deep-gas well and smokeless generator.

⛏

Away into the dark

World sites: sunken ruins, collapsed mines, sealed vaults, geothermal vents. At home: cave breakthroughs, gas firestorms, flood seeps, rich ore nodes, optional ancient stairwells.

💧

Fluid shafts

Soft-dep junctions for DBH, DCH, Rimatomics, VHGE, Rimefeller, and VEF chemfuel — meter across levels without merging pipe nets.

🚀

Gravship stacks (Odyssey)

Ship-only stairwells open A+/B+ pockets that travel on launch and reattach on landing. Colony dig/tower stairs stay planet-side.

🧭

One colony column

Work, food, rest, medical, joy, schedule, storage priority, construction pull, ritual escorts, caravan pull, raid pursuit — across every linked floor.

Shafts & power

The buildings you’ll place first.

Excavated / tower stairwell

Opens B− or A+. Ties power when wired. Seal to stop gas and raiders. Elevators do the same in 1×1 with a power gate on the paid direction.

Shaft power conduit

Optional dedicated tie away from the stair footprint — builds its partner below automatically.

Ventilation kit

Smoke hole (no research), louvers, exhaust fans, updraft filters, gas pipes. A working outlet keeps a room under harmful smoke.

Life support

O₂ / CO₂ pumps, shaft exchangers, canary cages. Medieval bellows and lime scrubbers if you’re not industrial yet.

Research

Own Strata tab. Medieval opens floors; industrial keeps them livable.

Digging down → B1 → Deep excavation → B2+, quest sites
+ Electricity → Elevators, Shaft power → Fluid shafts
Forced ventilation → Deep life support / Deep gas extraction
Rock shoring · Passive life support · Deep infrastructure · Deep agriculture · Mine atmosphere

Tips

  1. Leave pillars under thick rock roof — or research shoring.
  2. Seal a shaft to stop raiders and gas; it also traps your own people.
  3. Grow A+ by expanding roofs downstairs; open sky never holds buildings.
  4. On Odyssey ships, only gravship stairwells place on the footprint.

Installation

  1. Install Harmony.
  2. Download above; extract the Strata folder into RimWorld Mods.
  3. Enable Harmony, then Strata. Restart.
Saves: safe to add mid-save. Before removing: bring everyone up, haul what you care about, deconstruct all stairwells.
