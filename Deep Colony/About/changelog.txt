[h1]Deep Colony — 1.6.1[/h1]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Auto skill perks[/b] — perks unlock themselves at skill 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 (no points). If the skill falls below that, the perk lapses. Hediffs stay off Health unless you turn them on in settings. Skill-20 capstones default on.
[*] [b]Shooting accuracy[/b] — bonuses used to round to +0.0 on inspect. They now use the same scale as vanilla traits.
[/list]

[list]
[*] [b]Family tree close button[/b] — Rows / Pedigree on the Family tab no longer covers the inspect X.
[*] [b]Family tree Bio button[/b] — removed from the Bio card so it no longer covers Banish. Use the Family inspect tab.
[*] [b]Last of the line[/b] — opening a Strata floor no longer throws Collection was modified while scanning living colonists.
[/list]
