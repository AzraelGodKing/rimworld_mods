3.11.3
[h1]Strata - 3.11.3[/h1]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Several gravship tower elevators stay after travel.[/b] Extra tower elevators on the first floor of a gravship no longer vanish when you land on another map. Each elevator is kept and reconnected, including with Gravship Expanded Chapter 1.
[*] [b]Stair looping stops, then they work.[/b] After a material run they stay on that floor and do the job, instead of climbing back for another load. Sleep, hunger, recreation, and a drug need break the stair trip. The floor assignment is still there when the need is met.
[*] [b]Pets take the stairs home.[/b] A colony animal walks to its assigned bed or allowed area on another linked floor. Development mode can teleport a selected pawn to a linked floor, not only the current map.
[*] [b]Meditation spots on other floors.[/b] A pawn goes to their assigned meditation spot on any linked floor, and will use a free colonist spot upstairs or downstairs when this floor has none.
[/list]
