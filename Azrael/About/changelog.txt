[h1]Azrael — 1.0.0[/h1]

First release.

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Workshop preview[/b] - the Workshop image is Azrael's own overlook, not Homesteader's art.
[/list]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Series hub[/b] - Mod Options → Azrael lists loaded series mods and versions, DLC, build stamps, live soft-compat bridges, named conflicts, and failed patches. Copy report for support. Works from the main menu. EN/CN/RU.
[*] [b]Safe removal[/b] - Prepare to remove for each series mod lists leftover buildings, items, and pawn effects, opens with a one-line summary, and can queue deconstruction. Not a save scrubber. Any other active AzraelGodKing mod shows up with its own button.
[*] [b]The Deep Homestead[/b] scenario - 3 settlers, farm kit, Azrael storyteller. The default and Random starting site favor large hills or mountains. Strata and Homesteader start research when those mods are loaded. EN/CN/RU.
[*] [b]Azrael storyteller without Homesteader[/b] - the storyteller is added on its own when Homesteader is not loaded, with no duplicate when both are.
[*] [b]Series events under the Azrael storyteller[/b] - Living World refugees and warbands, Homesteader's Kats event, Stormproof weather, and Strata discoveries are picked more often when an event of their kind fires. How often events fire is unchanged, and big threats stay close to Cassandra. Other storytellers are unaffected.
[*] [b]Multiplayer notice[/b] - with the Multiplayer mod active, Conflicts warns that safe-removal deconstruct only acts on your client.
[*] [b]Update letter[/b] - loading a colony sends a letter with these notes and a link to the full changelog.
[/list]
