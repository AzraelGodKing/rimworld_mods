2.0.0
[h1]Deep Colony — 2.0.0[/h1]
Deep Colony 2 ships for both RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Your 1.6 colony keeps getting every update, and the same save carries over to 1.7.

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b] — Deep Colony's buttons and menu choices are synced when the Multiplayer mod is on. All players need the host's Deep Colony settings. Not tested yet.
[*] [b]Save upgrade[/b] — the first time an older save loads, Deep Colony cleans out entries for pawns that no longer exist. This can't be undone, so keep a backup if you might go back to 1.10.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]RimWorld 1.7[/b] — the 1.7 folder now has its own .NET 10 build made against the 1.7 game files. 1.6 keeps its own build from the same source.
[*] [b]Stormproof[/b] — ion-storm trauma asks Stormproof 2 directly instead of guessing from weather names.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Odyssey crash trauma[/b] now fires on gravship crashes, and no longer on unrelated "crash" incidents.
[*] [b]Save size[/b] — touch comfort is only tracked for your own colonists, prisoners and guests, and old entries are cleaned up daily.
[*] [b]Chronicle export[/b] — if the clipboard copy fails, the text file is still saved, and you are told if both fail.
[*] [b]Birth safety net[/b] — turning it off now turns off all of it, including the failed-birth recovery.
[*] [b]Ideology funerals[/b] now ease grief as intended. Before, the funeral was never recognised.
[*] [b]Loading a save[/b] can no longer apply a perk, heir, divorce, envoy, mentor or tribute click from the game you just left to someone in the new one.
[*] [b]Tribute[/b] no longer errors when you have no colony map, and takes the silver from a home map that has it.
[*] [b]Wary factions[/b] block trade caravans on about a third of days, as intended, instead of depending on how often the game re-checks.
[/list]
