[h1]Stormproof — 1.6.0[/h1]
[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] Load schedule shows the forecast: with a weather forecaster on the supply grid, each hour gets a bar where batteries dip (amber = brownout, orange = cutoff trips). Hover an hour for its lowest predicted charge.
[*] Weather almanac draws the next 8 hours of predicted battery charge for the forecaster's grid.
[*] Weather almanac shows the longest stretch without a missed strike or a suffered Zzzt, plus the current one.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] Almanac lifetime totals no longer shrink when old entries roll off. Existing saves start from the entries still in the almanac.
[*] Brownout strength can no longer leak from one power grid to another.
[*] Grid monitor "empty in" / "full in" no longer shows a garbled time on a nearly balanced grid.
[*] Weather forecaster and storm caller fail quietly instead of erroring if a RimWorld update changes the weather timer.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] Grid forecast follows load shedders: scheduled shed hours, holds, and the battery cutoff drop or restore the sub-grid's draw.
[/list]
