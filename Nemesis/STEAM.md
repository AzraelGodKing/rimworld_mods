## Short

A named pawn becomes your personal antagonist — taunts, false leads, sabotage, and assaults that flee when losing. Capture, kill, or outlast them… or watch them win.

## Long

**Nemesis** turns one hostile pawn into a persistent hunter with a name, a voice, and a memory.

They do not replace faction raids. They sit beside them: comms taunts, fake distress signals, caravan ambushes, food-store raids, EMP sabotage, sniper terror, kidnap attempts, and personal assaults that prioritize a fixation target then break for the rim when the fight turns.

### How it starts
- Kill an enemy's ally or kin
- Prisoner / slave escape vendettas
- Wounded-and-escaped returns
- Fixation after they kill one of yours

### How it ends
Capture (execute / release / keep / truce / recruit / ransom / bounty), corner them after enough escapes, or lose — if their target dies or is handed over, they can win.

### Depth
Archetypes, scar-aware taunts, calling cards, progressive camp intel (real / empty / trap), living truces, trophy ledger, difficulty presets, and soft compat with **Stormproof**, **Strata**, and **Homesteader**.

Requires Harmony. RimWorld 1.6. Safe mid-save add. Before removing: **Mod Settings → Resolve current hunt now**.
