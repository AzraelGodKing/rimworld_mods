[h1]Strata — 3.5.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Level purpose tags[/b] — Farm, Freezer, Workshop, Barracks, Quarry, Storage, Hospital, or none. Set from rename or the Levels tab. Matching tags tilt haul, rest, food, and work relays; untagged floors keep the old order. A wrong tag never blocks a job.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Wrong-floor bunk nap[/b] — while commuting to an assigned bed on another floor, GetRest no longer keeps a LayDown on an unoccupied bed on the current floor (sibling of the AZR-101 ground-sleep fix).
[*] [b]Dug floor stuck fog[/b] — mined underground cells that vanilla left fogged (no already-clear neighbor at destroy time) now flood-unfog; existing saves heal on load when the stuck fog borders clear space. Undug mineable rock still stays dark until mined.
[/list]
