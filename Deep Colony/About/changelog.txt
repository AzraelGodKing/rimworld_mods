[h1]Deep Colony — Feature Update[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Family join[/b] — kin on your map may join from a visit or defect from a raid. Spouse is the strongest pull; ex-lovers never join this way. Friendly family recruit does not anger their faction; enemies hold a grudge.
[*] [b]Ex-lovers[/b] — chance to get back together. After more than three reunions it is marked a toxic relationship that counseling can help.
[*] [b]Family tree[/b] — Family tab on the pawn inspect pane (next to Character). Click a relative to jump to that pawn. Also a Family tree button on the Bio card.
[*] [b]Family notes[/b] — first harvest as a family, marriage, coming of age, birthdays, and anniversaries on the Legacy tab. Eating with kin gives a short mood.
[*] Adult children returning to a parent's map get a reunion thought. Colony-born kids still keep "I grew up here" (not growth vats).
[*] [b]Tribute[/b] — Reputation tab (200 silver) or right-click silver / gold / jade / a valuable gift. Envoy on the map during a successful trade writes extra goodwill.
[*] Ideology funerals ease violent-loss trauma. Spouses get extra ease. Quiet rooms (no work benches) counsel better.
[*] Classroom extra when two apprentices share a Biotech blackboard. Right-click prisoner counseling (off by default).
[*] Odyssey isolation eases when you return to the surface. Strata gas / firestorm downs use existing trauma with a keyed reason.
[*] Perks tab archetype column. Reputation ally / hostile filter. Legacy lists traumatized colonists and remembrance names.
[*] Inspect: trauma types, teach progress, envoy, rival, and Date Night confidant count.
[*] Untreated-trauma alert, Perks filters, deathbed lesson, sibling bond, Royalty envoy bias, Date Night / Homesteader / Anomaly hooks (Batch C).
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] Rivalry checks no longer scan every colonist pair on a hot path.
[*] Deathbed lessons are player-side only. Funeral messages only fire if someone was actually eased.
[*] Counseling restarts the untreated-trauma grace. Birthday notes see siblings on caravans.
[*] Isekai Rank / destiny traits stay unique; birth inheritance only for player-side babies.
[/list]
