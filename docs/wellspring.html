Overview

RimWorld has always skipped the third pillar of frontier life. Homesteads grow food and burn fuel — Wellspring adds the water. Draw it, store it, and trench it into your fields for richer soil. Pairs naturally with Homesteader (water your orchard) and Stormproof, but requires neither.

No dependencies: currently fully XML-based — no Harmony, no C#. Safe to add mid-save. Water moves by colonist hauling for now; a proper fluid pipe system (pipes, powered pumps, sprinklers) is planned for a later release.

Buildings & terrain

The full water cycle: draw it, catch it, store it, spend it.

Hand-dug well

40 wood, 10 steel · Wellcraft research

A stone-lined well with a wooden windlass. Colonists draw 5 water per bill of honest labor — slow, reliable, and it works anywhere with ground under it.

Rain barrel

30 wood · Wellcraft research

Slowly collects rainwater and dew — 4 water every day or two, no labor required. Every roofline should have one.

Cistern

2×1 · 8 stacks of water · Wellcraft research

A covered stone tank that stockpiles water where the buckets can't spill and the sun can't reach it. Contents never deteriorate.

Deep well

30 wood, 60 steel · Waterworks research

A reinforced shaft sunk past the shallow groundwater. Each haul brings up 12 water per bill — serious volume for a serious colony.

Solar still

15 wood, 15 steel · Wellcraft research

Sweats moisture out of the ground drop by drop — 2 water every half-day or so, and it works even where it never rains. The desert colony's best friend.

Water tower

2×2 · 16 stacks of water · Waterworks research

A tall timber tank on stout legs holding a serious reserve out of the sun and the mud. Contents never deteriorate.

Stone fountain

2×2 · 120 stone + 10 water · +30 beauty

A carved basin with a gentle plume at its center. Serves no purpose except to remind everyone the colony has water to spare — which is exactly the point.

Irrigated planter

25 wood + 2 water · 130% fertility · Irrigation research

A deep planter box with a reservoir in its base — a little irrigated field you can put anywhere, even indoors.

Irrigated soil

3 water + labor per tile · +30% fertility · Irrigation research

Trench drawn water into cropland to work it into rich, dark growing soil. Only lays over ground that could already grow crops — you can't water a stone floor into a farm.

Made with water

Water isn't just for the fields.

Boiled water

1 water · any stove or campfire

The frontier's humblest drink — boiled clean, jugged warm, and worth a little joy to anyone who drinks it.

Mud bricks

2 water + 10 hay → 15 bricks · crafting spot or stonecutter

Sun-dried adobe: a cheap stony building material for walls, floors and furniture. Fireproof and quick to lay, but weaker than cut stone.

Clean bandages

1 water + 3 cloth → 2 bandages · 70% medical potency

Cloth strips boiled clean and rolled tight. Not true medicine, but a good deal better than a dirty shirt against the wound.

Installation

  1. Download with the button above and extract the Wellspring folder into your RimWorld Mods directory.
  2. Enable Wellspring in the mod list and restart the game.
Save compatibility: safe to add mid-save. Before removing the mod, deconstruct its buildings, use up its water, and remove any irrigated soil.
