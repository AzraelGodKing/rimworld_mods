Date Night

Schedule romance. Make memories.

Steam Workshop Download zip
Steam Workshop: — subscribers · — favorites

Overview

Adds Date and Lovin timetable slots to the Schedule tab. Date hours are a walk, a table, or hanging out — mood and opinion, no bed. Paint Lovin on both partners for the same hours and they meet in a shared double; lovin uses a short mean-time-between until the schedule changes. Adults without a partner in that bed can have some private time in any bed. Children never qualify.

Dependencies: requires Harmony. Load Date Night after it.
Save-safe: safe to add mid-save. Before removing, clear Date / Lovin slots back to Sleep / Anything.

How it works

1. Paint Date or Lovin

Open Schedule and pick the teal Date or rose Lovin button (vanilla hardcodes that grid, so XML alone never shows a custom slot). With Rimbody, Exosuit, or Schedule Everything, they sit in the next free extra columns so those mods keep Workout / Piloting.

2. Date hours

Assign the same hours on both lovers. They meet at a table, gathering spot, or just walk together. Mood and opinion only — no bed, no pregnancy.

3. Lovin hours

Vanilla partner-in-bed rules

Each paths to a shared rendezvous double (claimed for the window, then restored). When both are in it, lovin uses a short MTB. Ideology that forbids lovin, or Biotech sterile genes, reroute to a date instead. Adults without a partner in that bed can have private time. Toggle it off in mod settings. Children never qualify.

4. Show up

If a partner is drafted, in a medical bed, or off-map for the window, the one who waited gets a short stood-up thought. If both make it, they get a small boost. Hungry, chemical desire, player-forced jobs, and exhaustion still come first.

Settings

Pregnancy-safe cooldown

Default ON

Only shortens lovin chance while scheduled; keeps vanilla post-lovin cooldown so pregnancy rolls stay sane.

Eager mode

Optional

Also shortens post-lovin cooldown like Always Do Lovin. Can spam pregnancy rolls — leave off unless you want that.

Allow private time

Default ON

Adults on Lovin hours use any bed when a couple job cannot start. Mood thought only — no pregnancy. Same cooldown as lovin. Children never qualify.

Claim double for Lovin hours

Default ON

Temporarily assign the rendezvous double to the couple, then restore their previous beds when the window ends.

Requirements checklist

  • Date: love relationship, both scheduled Date, same map — adults only
  • Lovin: both scheduled Lovin, reachable shared double (claimed for the window)
  • Private time: adult colonist, Lovin hours, any reachable bed (toggle in settings)
  • Ideology / Biotech: no-lovin precepts and sterile genes reroute to Date
  • Works for no-sleep / low-rest pawns who never hit a Sleep block

Dev tools

With Dev mode on: Date Night menu — make selected pair lovers, click-to-pair, paint Lovin or Date all day, force lovin / private time / date now, log rendezvous bed.

Installation

  1. Download the zip above (or copy the DateNight folder into your RimWorld Mods directory).
  2. Enable Harmony, then Date Night, and restart.
  3. Open a colonist’s Schedule tab — Date and Lovin appear beside Sleep.
