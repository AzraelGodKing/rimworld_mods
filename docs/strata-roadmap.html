STRATA — V2 ROADMAP

Where the deep goes next

Community-driven Not committed scope Updated July 2026
← Back to Strata Suggest a feature
What this is: the planning backlog for Strata's next major version, shaped directly by player feedback. Nothing here is promised or dated — it's the honest list of what's being designed, in the order it's likely to land. Ideas credited where they came from the community. Got one? Use the button above.
Pillar 0

Polish & Hardening

Smaller quality-of-life items carried forward from V1 — slotted in between the big pushes.

📦

Smarter logistics

Haulers prefer the powered elevator for heavy loads, relays pick the shortest shaft path instead of the first one found, and mid-haul seal changes are handled gracefully.

⚠

Threat telegraphing

Tremors warn before a deep raid erupts, raiders besiege sealed shafts instead of shrugging, and a depth-scaled threat pass makes richer ore mean nastier bugs.

🔔

Alerts & helpers

Warnings for smoke building on an empty level and colonists stuck below a sealed shaft; placement arrows for fan intakes and duct exits; ritual support for prisoners and animals.

🛠

Under the hood

Unit tests for the level graph and smoke math, a proper README, one raid = one story coordination for multi-level pursuits, and dedicated art for the smoke hole and updraft filter.

Pillar 1 — idea credit: Thundercraft & dognamedKats

The Living Deep

The deep becomes a place with resources and an atmosphere — not just rock to hollow out.

♨

Hidden geothermal chambers

deeper is richer · found by mining

Steam geysers seeded in small fogged chambers sealed in the rock — your miners discover them the way they discover ore. The vanilla geothermal generator just works, and its heat pours into your tunnels through the existing temperature exchange.

☢

A real gas system

built on the smoke engine

The smoke simulation generalizes into a full atmosphere: toxic and flammable gases that pool, flow through doors and vents, and respect seals — every ventilation tool you already own works on them.

🔥

Flammable pockets

torches become mining hazards

Crack into a gas pocket and the room fills. Bring an open flame into a saturated room and it ignites — electric lighting underground stops being a luxury and becomes safety equipment.

⚙

Gas as a resource

vent it for safety, or tap it for power

A gas well on a deep vent feeds a deep-gas generator — no dependencies needed. Running Vanilla Helixien Gas Expanded? Deep vents can feed its pipe network instead. Venting a gas-filled room keeps your pawns safe but wastes the fuel: your call.

Pillar 2 — idea credit: Hat

Fluid Shafts

Water, oil, gas, paste, and chemfuel between levels — without touching any pipe mod's network internals.

💧

The power-shaft trick, applied to pipes

no network merging · no jank · negligible performance cost

Strata never splices pipe networks across maps — that's the fragile approach. Instead: a paired shaft node on each level, each an ordinary member of its own network, with a demand-driven metered transfer between them. It's a pump through the shaft, not a spliced pipe — the same proven design as Strata's power ties.

🔧

Dubs Bad Hygiene

Water and sewage across levels — plus groundwater seeded on Strata levels so underground wells work at all. Deeper digs tap richer aquifers.

🔧

Vanilla Expanded pipes

One adapter covers the whole VE family: nutrient paste, Helixien gas, chemfuel pipes, and resource tanks.

🔧

Rimefeller

Oil and the chem network, same treatment. All adapters are soft dependencies — nothing breaks without them.

Pillar 3

Rival Raiders

The deep remembers — and so do the people who crawled out of it.

⚔

Escaped raiders hold grudges

A raider who escapes your colony is remembered. Seasons later they return leading their own raid — wearing the scar you gave them, named in the letter, and heading straight for the stairwell they escaped through. Kill them and the story closes; let them slip away again and the third visit is worse.

Pillar 4 — the big one

Building Up

Above-ground floors. Sky is the new rock.

🏗

Floors that grow with your base

rooftop solar · tower greenhouses · sky bedrooms

An upper level is a full map of open sky — unbuildable void except directly above supported structure. Just as mining expands a level below, building expands the level above: every wall you raise on the ground floor extends the buildable footprint upstairs. Cell-for-cell alignment with the floor below, real weather and sunlight up top, and floors that collapse if you demolish what holds them up.

☀

Real sky

Upper floors get genuine sunlight and surface weather: solar panels on the roof, greenhouses without sun lamps — and yes, drop pods can land on your rooftop.

⇅

Up and down, one base

Combined with the underground levels, Strata becomes the complete vertical-base mod: sky gardens to deep vaults, one colony, many floors.
