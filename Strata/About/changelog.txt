3.3.1
[h1]Strata — 3.3.1[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Elevator call, hold, and priority (AZR-64)[/b] — gizmos on both landings. Hold keeps the car off transit hops; priority 1–5 steers automatic routing.
[*] [b]Level stamp (AZR-61)[/b] — Stamp on the Levels tab copies walls, doors, floors, and stockpiles onto a linked floor as blueprints. Rotate first.
[*] [b]Support overlay (AZR-63)[/b] — play-settings toggle marks excavated cells past a roof holder or pillar. Cave-ins prefer those cells.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Sleep on the ground (AZR-101)[/b] — assigned beds on another floor no longer lose to a dirt nap when the stair commute fails.
[*] [b]Paramedic stair hauls (AZR-100)[/b] — mechanoids no longer auto-export items through stairs. Cargo is not dropped on a pawn.
[*] [b]Undug rock fog (AZR-57)[/b] — mining no longer lights neighboring unmined rock. Walk the tunnel to see it.
[*] [b]Harmony JobDriver Cleanup[/b] — cross-level jobs no longer log patch errors on inherited Cleanup. Stair jobs still work.
[*] [b]Level stamp crash (AZR-122)[/b] — stamping a roof-deck or gravship floor no longer throws. Non-buildable terrain is skipped; a failed stamp closes the window instead of erroring every frame.
[/list]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Update letter[/b] — loading a colony shows this version's notes from About/changelog.txt, with a link to the newest full notes.
[/list]

3.2.0
[h1]Strata — 3.2.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Room air on the stats card[/b] — oxygen, carbon dioxide, and smoke with fine / stale / dangerous stages when you inspect a room.
[*] [b]Multi-floor incompat[/b] — the mod list flags AASB, AASB 2, and MultiFloors. A letter names them if you load anyway.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Verbose log[/b] — routine Player.log chatter is off unless you enable Verbose log in settings. Startup line and real errors stay.
[*] [b]See-below DrawPos[/b] — Harmony patches on Thing.DrawPos apply only while see-below is on, and unpatch when you turn it off.
[*] [b]Smoke auto-tag[/b] — only a named vanilla/DLC list gets smoke comps in C#. Other mods' stoves and generators are not tagged by heuristic.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Root cellar rot[/b] — underground Homesteader root-cellar cooling no longer shares Harmony prefix/postfix state between rotting items.
[*] [b]Save-load statics[/b] — session caches (hibernate, cages, threat letters, off-thread air jobs, pipe nets, and the rest) clear when you load a different save.
[*] [b]Translations[/b] — dropped leftover gravship Keyed strings from Chinese/Russian; Russian gravship life-support buildings now have DefInjected labels.
[/list]

[b]Performance[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Map kind[/b] — underground / roof-deck / depth lookups are cached per map instead of string-comparing biomes on every temperature tick.
[*] [b]Open roofs[/b] — unroofed-cell counts on Strata floors are cached until a roof changes.
[*] [b]Atmosphere[/b] — breath diffusion reuses per-map buffers; cloud-key snapshots no longer allocate a new list each cycle.
[*] [b]Hibernate check[/b] — vacant-level pawn scans are cached for about a second.
[/list]
