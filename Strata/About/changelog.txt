[h1]Strata - 3.11.4[/h1]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Orbital trade across floors[/b] (AZR-691) - a comms console on any linked floor sees orbital beacons and stockpiles on every other linked floor. Bought goods land on the surface (or another open-sky floor) instead of vanishing under mountain roof. Sold stacks are taken from those beacons, not only the console's map.
[*] [b]Pets walk the stairs during the day[/b] (AZR-649) - colony animals use wander, not the colonist "leave this cell" job, so they now take the stairs to an assigned bed or allowed area on another floor while wandering.
[*] [b]Meditation spots that are not the vanilla pad[/b] (AZR-650) - anima trees, shrines, and other meditation foci on linked floors count. A pawn on a Meditate schedule leaves work to go there.
[/list]
