1.3.0
[h1]Stormproof — 1.3.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Load schedule[/b] — the load shedder can run or shed by hour of the day, shed early when a storm or flare is about to land, and Hold Auto / Run / Shed in one click. The battery cutoff still trips first. Old saves keep the old breaker until you set a schedule.
[*] [b]Almanac ledger[/b] — each recorded storm tracks strikes caught vs missed, Zzzt absorbed vs suffered, fires the suppressor snuffed, and wear hits on unhardened wiring.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Building art[/b] — Power-tab sprites redrawn and sized at 128 px per cell so tall buildings are no longer squashed. Storm caller, sky restorer, fire suppressor, and drought condenser match their footprints.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Grid forecast[/b] — empty-in no longer trusts brownout-dimmed draw; night forecasts no longer halve the next sunrise; selecting a monitor no longer recomputes every frame; alarms re-arm from current charge; a full grid shows held charge instead of "full in 4 minutes".
[*] [b]XML load[/b] — the files that crashed some Nexus installs are well-formed UTF-8 with no BOM.
[/list]
