[h1]Deep Colony — 1.6.2[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]God-mode Family tab[/b] — with DevMode and god mode on, right-click the Family tab title or a relative to bring living kin onto this map as colonists. Dead kin are skipped. Also in the Deep Colony debug palette.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Mid-save install[/b] — rivalry, touch-need, family life/echo, and ex-lover reconcile now tick when you add the mod to an existing colony (they used to wait on a counter that never lined up with the game clock).
[*] [b]Harmony patches[/b] — one failed patch logs and skips instead of unloading the rest of Deep Colony.
[*] [b]Mentor / counsel jobs[/b] — Harmony no longer treats vanilla JobDriver.Cleanup as a patch, so mentoring and counseling load without an error.
[*] [b]Last of the line[/b] — death / recruit / faction-change scans no longer throw Collection was modified if a pawn list mutates mid-pass (AZR-94).
[/list]

1.6.1
[h1]Deep Colony — 1.6.1[/h1]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Auto skill perks[/b] — perks unlock themselves at skill 5 / 10 / 15 / 20 (no points). If the skill falls below that, the perk lapses. Hediffs stay off Health unless you turn them on in settings. Skill-20 capstones default on.
[*] [b]Shooting accuracy[/b] — bonuses used to round to +0.0 on inspect. They now use the same scale as vanilla traits.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Family tree close button[/b] — Rows / Pedigree on the Family tab no longer covers the inspect X.
[*] [b]Family tree Bio button[/b] — removed from the Bio card so it no longer covers Banish. Use the Family inspect tab.
[*] [b]Last of the line[/b] — opening a Strata floor no longer throws Collection was modified while scanning living colonists.
[/list]
