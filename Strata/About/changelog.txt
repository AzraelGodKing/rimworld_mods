3.11.0
[h1]Strata - 3.11.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Guards For Me.[/b] A standing guard walks to a post on another floor, and that post can take a guard from a linked floor. A bodyguard follows their person through the stairs and stays assigned when that person changes floors. A patrol continues to a waypoint on another floor. A reaction force takes the stairs toward a threat on a linked floor. Guards on duty are not pulled away by work relay.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Jobs survive a level change.[/b] Shift-clicked orders are all kept and started again on arrival. If the first one cannot start, or ruin stairs draft the pawn on the way in, the order is retried. A work trip keeps looking for that work type, including mining and other cell jobs. Queued jobs for the floor they left resume back there. Orders still in flight are saved with the colony.
[*] [b]Work relay.[/b] An idle pawn goes to the linked floor where their own best job is, then the nearest of those. Benches and switches count. On arrival they search that work the way the game does, including nearby designations. A fire check no longer clears the arrival check, and a floor that just had nothing does not pull them straight back.
[/list]
