## Soft-compat web

- [ ] Formalize soft-compat **both directions** (fail-open). Examples:
  - Storm surges flood unpumped Strata levels
  - Drought condenser → Homesteader cisterns
  - Strata underground grid is ion-immune but needs a surface antenna for comms
  - Living World chronicle → Deep Colony goodwill drift; Nemesis hunt escalate/dormant; Homesteader flavor lines

## Storyteller & showcase

- [ ] **Azrael** series storyteller — weights incidents from Strata / Homesteader / Stormproof / Nemesis, and later Living World fallout incidents (refugees, warbands) at low threat cost
- [ ] Showcase scenario **“The Deep Homestead”** — river, storm-heavy, core four mods (+ optional Deep Colony / Living World when those exist)
