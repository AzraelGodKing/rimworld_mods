2.0.0
[h1]Nemesis — 2.0.0[/h1]
Nemesis 2 ships for both RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Your 1.6 colony keeps getting every update, and the same save carries over to 1.7.

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Several nemeses at once[/b] — each hostile faction can send its own nemesis, up to a limit you set (default 2). A cooldown between new hunts stops one bad raid from creating several, and two nemeses never raid you within half a day of each other. The dossier has a button per hunt.
[*] [b]Captain AI[/b] — the nemesis goes for their target first, kidnaps only that target, and pulls back when the raid falls apart. Their raid breaches, besieges, or waits behind a commander depending on their combat style.
[*] [b]Odyssey shuttles[/b] — with Odyssey, a nemesis sometimes lands by shuttle and flies out on it when beaten back.
[*] [b]Warcaskets[/b] — with Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates, a seasoned Destroyer or Survivor brings a warcasket.
[*] [b]Hunt camps[/b] — a good informant lead marks the nemesis's camp on the world map. Send a caravan to catch them there, but some leads are decoys.
[*] [b]Sister mods[/b] — with Living World, a nemesis reacts when their faction is wiped out or wins or loses a war. With Stormproof, they strike during ion storms. With Strata, a deep raid in your cellars counts as "already under attack".
[*] [b]Presets[/b] — Relentless, Default and Slow-burn buttons at the top of the settings.
[*] [b]You can spot them[/b] — a red aura that grows with the nemesis's level, gear dyed in their combat style's colour, a real calling card, and a scrawled mark where they strike.
[*] [b]The bounty matters[/b] — while posted it buys better informants (fewer false leads). It is paid out when you kill or capture the nemesis, and returned by drop pod if the hunt ends any other way.
[*] [b]Settings[/b] — how much calmer a nemesis returns after a truce, the health at which a cornered nemesis gives up, and how many escapes before colonists get upset.
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b] — Nemesis's buttons are synced when the Multiplayer mod is on. All players need the host's Nemesis settings. Not tested yet.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Dossier[/b] — after an escape it points at the nemesis faction's home instead of your colony, and notes switch language with the game.
[*] [b]Comms console[/b] — replying to a nemesis or buying a lead now sends the colonist to the console first, and the options only show when the console can be used.
[*] [b]Saves[/b] — your 1.x hunt carries over to 2.0 automatically. A 2.0 save does not load on older Nemesis versions.
[*] [b]RimWorld 1.7[/b] — the 1.7 folder now has its own .NET 10 build made against the 1.7 game files. 1.6 keeps its own build from the same source.
[*] [b]Stormproof[/b] — EMP sabotage asks Stormproof whether a building is covered by a dampener, using its real radius and power. A surge protector that is still recharging no longer blocks the extra short circuit.
[*] [b]Quieter hunt schedule[/b] — comms taunts need a console that can call right now. Some picks are a deliberate quiet spell.
[*] [b]Performance[/b] — less work on every pawn death and while the dossier is open, and faster calls into Strata, Homesteader and Deep Colony.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Raid fallback[/b] — a nemesis walked in by hand when the raid missed them now attacks instead of standing idle.
[*] [b]Freed captive[/b] — a captive freed off the map, or a nemesis who walks off the edge, stays tracked instead of being cleaned up and ending the hunt.
[*] [b]Hunt lifecycle[/b] — a nemesis off the home map can still slip away or be killed for real, a missing captain ends the hunt, ending a hunt frees the world pawn, and a rogue captain only joins a hostile human faction.
[/list]
