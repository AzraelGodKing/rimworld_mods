Overview

Off-map faction life: settlements drift, flip ownership, feud, and make news you hear through rumor, proximity, or radio — without a second storyteller dumping threat at you.

Dependencies: requires Harmony.
Lanes: personal antagonists stay in Nemesis; player goodwill stays in Deep Colony; homestead flavor stays in Homesteader.
Save-safe: safe to add mid-save.

Shipped (Phase 1–2)

Chronicle

Hear-rules

Major events always; medium/high gated by contact, proximity, allies, and comms.

Settlement morph

Prosperity / flip / abandon

Outposts, epithets, and inspector cues as the world map quietly changes.

Diplomacy

Peace → war

NPC faction pairs feud, white-peace, and rarely ally; wars leave refugee fallout.

War sites

Short-lived

Generic war pins on the world map; optional warbands stay settings-gated.

Installation

  1. Download the zip above (or copy the LivingWorld folder into your RimWorld Mods directory).
  2. Enable Harmony, then Living World, and restart.
