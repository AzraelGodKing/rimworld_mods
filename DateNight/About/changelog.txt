[h1]Date Night — 1.0.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Date activities[/b] — dates pick a real activity from the map (dinner, picnic, walk, stargazing, dancing, gift, recreation) instead of only hanging at a gather spot; shared couple+day seed so both partners match. Off in settings.
[*] [b]Date quality[/b] — wonderful / nice / awkward outcomes from activity, beauty, weather, and a shared roll. Off in settings.
[*] [b]Ruined date[/b] — draft, mental break, or active hostiles after a date starts leave a short negative mood.
[*] [b]Post-date lovin spark[/b] — a finished date shortens the couple's lovin wait for about a day. Off in settings.
[*] Schedule mismatch alert when lovers paint different Date / Lovin hours.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Date hours idle apart[/b] — both partners share one venue for every date activity (walk, picnic, stargaze, dinner, dance, hangout, gift, recreation) and stay together; the follower looks at their partner instead of freezing one way; dates are not interrupted by casual work. — Date sits last after Clean (Lovin in the ▾ menu) and no longer covers Manage areas.
[*] [b]Lovin animation loop[/b] — with animation mods (Rimworld Animations, breeding ritual, RJW), Date Night no longer restarts lovin every couple of seconds. One animation plays through; a new one starts only after it finishes if Lovin hours are still on. Pregnancies can complete again.
[*] [b]Date hours crash[/b] — no longer throws when joy AI runs on a Date timetable slot (vanilla GetPriority only knows Joy / Sleep / etc.).
[/list]
