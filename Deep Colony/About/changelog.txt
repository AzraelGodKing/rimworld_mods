[h1]Deep Colony — 1.6.7[/h1]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Failed birth no longer wedges the mother[/b] — if the baby fails to generate, labor ends instead of throwing every tick. Teardown exceptions are swallowed, stuck labor is force-detached, and load plus mid-session sweeps recover already-wedged pawns.
[*] [b]Birth safety net holds under race mods[/b] — the newborn prefix runs first (before HAR) and is re-applied if Harmony skipped it. Player.log now prints version, assembly, stamp, and whether the prefix is actually applied.
[*] [b]Trauma ticks fail-open[/b] — one colonist cannot take down the trauma sweep. Combat-habit hediffs are not added while downed.
[/list]
