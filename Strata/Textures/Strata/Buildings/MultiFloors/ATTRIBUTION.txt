Optional alternate stair and elevator art for Strata's "Multifloor Stairs" mod setting.

Textures in HandrailStairsMedium/ and ModernElevator/ are adapted from the
MultiFloors mod by Telardo (Steam Workshop). Telardo may request removal.

Strata does not require MultiFloors to be installed. These copies live under
Strata/Buildings/MultiFloors/ and are only used when the setting is enabled.
