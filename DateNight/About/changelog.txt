[h1]Date Night — 1.6.0[/h1]
[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Milestone anniversaries[/b] — every fifth year together gets its own letter and a bigger, longer-lasting mood.
[*] [b]Double-date rapport[/b] — couples who get along pick each other again; couples who keep clashing stop being paired.
[*] [b]Reunions[/b] — partners apart for two days or more (caravan, kidnapping, another map) get a "reunited" mood when they're back together.
[*] [b]Breakups[/b] — a breakup or divorce gives a short "no more date nights" mood and forgets the couple's favourite date spot.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Thoughtful gifts[/b] — no gifts the receiver would refuse: no drinks or drugs for teetotalers, nothing their drug policy or ideoligion forbids, no food they wouldn't eat.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Double date no-show[/b] — the "double date fell through" mood is adults-only like every other Date Night mood.
[*] [b]Dev tools[/b] — "Force private time" no longer errors on a pawn without an age tracker.
[/list]
