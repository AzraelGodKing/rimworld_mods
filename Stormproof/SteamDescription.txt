[h1]Stormproof[/h1]
The Rim does not care about your freezer. Solar flares, lightning, surges, EMP, toxic fallout, heat, cold, eclipses, flashstorms, droughts — Stormproof is the Power-tab toolkit that keeps a colony running through all of it.

[b]Version 1.4.0.[/b] RimWorld 1.6. Requires [b]Harmony[/b]. Odyssey optional (drought condenser). Safe to add mid-save.

[h2]What's new in 1.4.0[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Hard forecast severity[/b] — the grid monitor's 8-hour projection uses the full brownout range (up to Hard), matching live brownout.
[*] [b]Storm caller queue[/b] — discharge queues for the next map tick instead of mutating weather from the UI gizmo.
[/list]

[h2]Grid defense[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Solar shield[/b] — keeps every electrical device on the map running during a flare. Idles cheap; drinks 2,500W while the sky is red. If batteries empty mid-flare, the shield collapses.
[*] [b]Storm spire[/b] — steals lightning in a wide radius (shown on select and while placing), cannot burn, and snuffs fires at its base. Wired: each strike dumps up to 1,500 Wd into capacitors then batteries, with a small "Zzzt!" risk until you research perfect grounding. Unwired: just a safe rod.
[*] [b]Storm capacitor bank[/b] — lightning-only storage. The grid cannot charge it; "Zzzt!" cannot drain it; it never leaks. Covers deficits up to 2,000W.
[*] [b]Surge protector[/b] — eats one "Zzzt!" then spends a day recharging.
[*] [b]EMP dampener[/b] — colony buildings in range shrug off EMP stuns. Turrets keep firing. Does not protect pawns or mechanoids.
[*] [b]Armored conduit[/b] — fireproof, tough, and immune to storm wear — the wiring for killboxes and exterior walls.
[*] [b]Load shedder[/b] — automatic breaker between the main grid and a low-priority sub-grid. Trips at an adjustable cutoff so the freezer outlasts the television. Optional 24-hour run/shed schedule, forecast override before a storm or flare, and Hold Auto / Run / Shed. Battery cutoff still wins.
[*] [b]Grid monitor[/b] — live production, consumption, battery time-to-empty, brownout level, and charge warnings. With a powered weather forecaster on the same net, an 8-hour weather-aware forecast that models brownout through Hard severity.
[/list]

[h2]Grid stress[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Graded brownout[/b] — when batteries fall below 40%, lights dim, workbenches slow, and coolers lag — draw drops so the remaining charge lasts longer. A load shedder still cuts the sub-grid at its trip point.
[*] [b]Storm wear[/b] — thunder, ion storms, and high wind slowly chip ordinary conduit and batteries. Armored conduit and storm capacitor banks are immune. Repair as usual.
[/list]

[h2]Atmosphere & weather[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Weather forecaster[/b] — how long this weather holds, incoming thunderstorms, an hour's warning before it breaks, and an almanac of this map's storms and hazards — with a ledger of strikes caught vs missed, Zzzt absorbed vs suffered, fires snuffed, and wear hits.
[*] [b]Fallout scrubber[/b] — indoor detox for pawns and animals in the enclosed room (room outline on select). Useless outdoors.
[*] [b]Storm caller[/b] — summon a rainy thunderstorm on demand (half-day storm, five-day recharge) — feeds spires and douses wildfires.
[*] [b]Static discharge pylon[/b] — stuns and burns raiders and mechanoids on bottled lightning from a storm capacitor. No capacitor charge, no zap.
[/list]

[h2]Hazard hardening (Spacer)[/h2]
Late-game map-wide counters. Idle cheap; pull hard while the event is on. No stored energy, no protection.
[list]
[*] [b]Atmospheric barrier[/b] — toxic fallout, toxic surge, volcanic ash, noxious haze (map-wide while powered).
[*] [b]Climate stabilizer[/b] — heat wave, cold snap, volcanic winter, heat dome, polar front.
[*] [b]Sky restorer[/b] — usable daylight during eclipses, volcanic winters, ash, and darkened skies (map-wide while powered).
[*] [b]Fire suppressor[/b] — extinguishes fires in a wide radius (flashstorm / dry lightning); radius shown on select and while placing.
[*] [b]Drought condenser[/b] — cancels drought plant-growth penalties (Odyssey). Harmless furniture if droughts never arrive.
[/list]

[h2]Events[/h2]
Ion storm, dry lightning front, heat dome, polar front, and toxic surge — each with a matching counter. Vanilla flares, fallout, flashstorms, heat/cold, eclipse, volcanic winter, and drought are covered once hazard hardening is online.

An ion storm bleeds batteries, pops EMP bursts, and extra "Zzzt!" shorts. Dampeners, surge protectors, and storm capacitors are the answer.

[h2]Scenario[/h2]
[b]Stormfront[/b]: a hard-weather showcase start that unlocks storm protection research.

[h2]Research[/h2]
Electricity → storm protection → flare shielding → atmospheric control / perfect grounding → hazard hardening. Dedicated Stormproof research tab.

[h2]Settings[/h2]
Soft / Default / Hard presets. Per-incident toggles and frequency, suppressor toggles, brownout strength (Hard pushes forecast and live brownout harder), storm wear, almanac, fulgurite, and spire "Zzzt!" chance.

[h2]Around the yard[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Fulgurite[/b] — a well-struck spire fuses glass at the base every eight strikes — trade good and sculpture stuff.
[*] [b]Storm vane[/b] — a copper weather vane. It predicts nothing. Every proper homestead has one.
[/list]

[h2]Which building?[/h2]
[list]
[*] [b]Spire vs solar shield[/b] — the spire is lightning and fire. The shield is flares. You usually want both.
[*] [b]Capacitor vs battery[/b] — capacitors only fill from spires and survive surges. Batteries run the rest of the colony.
[*] [b]Barrier vs scrubber[/b] — the barrier holds the sky; the scrubber clears toxin already in the blood. Pair them.
[*] [b]Forecaster vs vane[/b] — the forecaster is an instrument. The vane is decoration.
[/list]
