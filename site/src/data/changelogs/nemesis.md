# Nemesis — Changelog

Foundation by **Dredd (Misakabob)** — original design, persistent antagonist pawn, escape/capture loop, aggression pacing, assaults, waste drops, fixation/prison-break triggers, resolution dialog, and settings. Credited with gratitude; this monorepo package extends that work.

## [Unreleased]

- **Fixed: invisible pawns when a nemesis raids** (AZR-742) — the red aura no longer draws while vanilla bakes the shared pawn texture atlas. Baking a MoteGlow node with immediate `DrawMeshNow` could bleed into other atlas cells and leave the cache camera rect unrestored (everyone invisible, map edge flickering). Live (zoomed-in) draws still show the aura; MaterialPropertyBlock tinting is guarded so a failure never suppresses vanilla pawn drawing.
- Docs: ROADMAP checks off 2.0 items that already ship (warcaskets, tactic matrix, hunt camps, multi-faction hunts, tint/aura, hunt LordJob, Odyssey shuttle, Living World listen). Dropped the deleted Strata V4 roadmap link.

## [2.0.0]

Player-facing version **2.0.0** (`About.xml` `modVersion`). Stamp `nemesis2-net10`. Ships for RimWorld **1.6** and **1.7** from the same source; 1.6 is not frozen.

- **RimWorld 1.7 build** (AZR-519) — the `1.7` folder gets its own .NET 10 DLL built against the 1.7 game files and Harmony 3. The `1.6` folder keeps its .NET Framework DLL. Both are built from the same source.
- **Several nemeses at once** (AZR-537) — each hostile faction can run its own hunt, up to **Hunts at once** (default 2, 1-3). A new hunt cannot start within **Days between new hunts** (default 3) of the last one, so one bad raid no longer spawns several. Two hunts never raid you within half a day of each other. Letters show the nemesis name in their faction's colour. The dossier gets a button per hunt.
- **Save upgrade** (AZR-552) — a 1.x save's single hunt (active, truce, or waiting on the capture dialog) loads into the new hunt list with its bounty, notes and epitaphs. The last known tile now keeps its planet layer, and the nemesis and target are saved as references next to their IDs. On the first 2.0 load, finished nemeses that 1.x left pinned as world pawns are released. A 2.0 save does not load back on 1.x.
- **Multiplayer** (AZR-553, AZR-658) — the health check runs on a fixed 120-tick cadence instead of faster on the map you are looking at, so clients never fire escapes on different ticks. Dossier buttons and the capture dialog go through `NemesisPlayerCommand`, registered as Multiplayer sync methods by reflection when the Multiplayer mod is on. Comms console replies are now a job: the colonist walks to the console first, like a vanilla call, and the options only show when vanilla's own options are usable (power, path, able to talk). Mod settings are not synced; every player needs the host's settings. Not tested in a two-client session yet.
- **NemesisCompatApi 2** — multi-hunt aware: `EngagedHuntCount`, `EngagedNemesisPawns()`, `LastHuntThreatTick`, `ClearAllHunts()`. `IsNemesisPawn` now means any engaged hunt (active, truce or captured) everywhere.
- **Bounty does something** (AZR-657, AZR-662) — the bounty belongs to its hunt. While posted it buys better informants: a 600-silver bounty cuts false leads from a quarter of rolls to about 6% and makes a tile sighting likelier. When the hunt ends in your favour (killed, captured, died in custody) it is paid out to the informants; any other end sends it back by drop pod.
- **Truce is a breather** (AZR-661) — when a truce ends the nemesis returns with aggression lowered by **Aggression lost after a truce** (default 2, never below 1).
- **New settings** (AZR-707) — **Cornered nemesis gives up below X% health** (was a fixed 45%) and **Escapes before colonists get upset** (was a fixed 2).
- **Fixed: lordless raid fallback** (AZR-659) — when the raid group misses the nemesis and they are walked in by hand, they get their own assault lord instead of standing idle.
- **Fixed: freed captive** (AZR-660) — a captive freed off-map is parked as a world nemesis like every other escape. A nemesis who walks off a map edge or is moved by another mod is re-pinned as a kept world pawn, so world-pawn cleanup cannot quietly end the hunt.
- **Fixed: captured end** (AZR-705) — ending a hunt as captured (API or another mod) sends a letter and frees the world pawn instead of skipping both.
- **1.7 patch safety** (AZR-534) — every Harmony patch binds game arguments by position (`__0`, `__1`, `__4`) instead of by name, and the social-fight patch checks the private `pawn` field before patching. The slave rebellion trigger patches the overload that builds the letter and only counts a rebellion that started. All targets were checked against the 1.6.4871 and 1.7.4998 reference assemblies.
- **Performance** (AZR-535) — the game component is cached instead of searched for on every pawn death; the five `Pawn.Kill` patches are one prefix and one postfix; Strata, Homesteader and Deep Colony are called through typed delegates bound once to their public APIs (no per-call `object[]` or boxing; Strata through `StrataModderApi`, Deep Colony through `FamilyLetterUtility.RecordNote` so its own trim rules apply); the surface home map is cached for 250 ticks; a missing nemesis or target costs at most one full pawn scan per 2,500 ticks; defs are `[DefOf]` instead of looked up by name; the dossier rebuilds its text twice a second instead of every frame. The source is nullable-annotated and both builds compile without warnings. Not profiled in game yet.
- **Captain AI** (AZR-554) — a nemesis assault now runs its own hunt lord. The captain goes for their fixation target first and, on a kidnap assault, carries off only that target (vanilla took any downed colonist). Mounts, mechs and warcaskets escort the captain. When most of the raid is down, dead or gone, everyone pulls back with a message. The supporting raid follows a tactic picked by combat focus: **Breach** (Destroyer, Berserker) breaks walls, **Siege** (Sniper) digs in while the captain hunts from the flank, **Commander** (Psycho, Mechanitor) holds the captain at the landing point until the raid is worn down or about two hours pass, and **Assault** (Survivor) is a plain attack. Each tactic has a dev-mode action. The raid fallback lord from AZR-659 is a hunt lord too.
- **Warcaskets** (AZR-554, soft) — with Vanilla Factions Expanded - Pirates, a level 5+ Destroyer or Survivor brings one warcasket of their own faction (any if it has none). Uses the **Soft mech and warcasket escorts** setting.
- **Odyssey shuttle drop and extract** (AZR-706) — with Odyssey, a captain from an industrial or higher faction lands by shuttle 40% of the time (setting **Shuttle drops**). The shuttle waits at the landing spot; when the raid falls apart the captain runs back to it and flies out, kept as the same nemesis. If the shuttle cannot land, they walk in as before.
- **Series hub** (AZR-588) — `Nemesis.NemesisBuildInfo.BuildStamp` is public for Azrael's hub.
- **Hunt camps and false leads** (AZR-555) — a good informant lead now places a camp of the nemesis's faction on the world map, a few tiles from their nearest settlement. Send a caravan: on a real lead the nemesis is there with the camp's defenders (if they are not out raiding you); on a false lead the camp holds only their people and a calling card. Both look the same until you arrive, and camps time out after 12 days. The world object def is `Nemesis_HuntSite` (a copy of vanilla's site def), so Living World keeps its war sites off it. After an escape the dossier points at the faction's nearest settlement instead of your own colony, and the dossier jump goes to the camp. Dev-mode actions create real and false camps.
- **Notes follow your language** (AZR-555) — dossier notes are saved as translation keys and arguments and translated when shown. Notes from 1.x saves keep their old text.
- **Living World** (AZR-556, AZR-722) — Nemesis listens to Living World's world news. If a hunt's faction collapses, the nemesis has nothing left to lose (aggression +2, a move within a day), or with **Faction collapse makes the nemesis worse** off, goes quiet for 15 days. A decisive victory emboldens the winning faction's nemesis; a defeat delays the losing one by a day.
- **Stormproof ion-storm baiting** (AZR-556) — during an ion storm on your home map, an aggressive nemesis moves within a few hours (once per storm) and favours raids and assaults over sabotage. Setting **Strike during ion storms**.
- **Strata deep raids** (AZR-556) — the "wait while a big hostile force is already here" check counts hostiles on every linked Strata level, through `StrataModderApi.ReachableMaps`.
- **Presets and compat matrix** (AZR-572) — **Relentless**, **Default** and **Slow-burn** buttons at the top of the settings. An action weight of 0 shows as "off". `loadAfter` now lists Deep Colony, Living World, Azrael and VFE Pirates. `Nemesis/COMPATIBILITY.md` lists every integration, its fail-open behaviour and the Multiplayer stance. The trigger-rate playtest is still to do.
- **Nemesis visuals** (AZR-536) — a red aura behind the nemesis, larger and brighter from level 1 to 8 (`Nemesis_BattleHardened` render node, vanilla glow shader, no Harmony). From level 3 their dyeable gear takes their combat style's colour. The calling card has its own art, the Scrawl mark paints a `Nemesis_Scrawl` filth, and the arranged-corpse mark leaves the scrawl in a ring of blood. Setting **Nemesis visuals** hides the aura and stops new dyeing. No custom shader: a vanilla glow is safer on both 1.6 and 1.7.
- **Translation check** (AZR-573) — ModChecks now also checks keys built with string interpolation (`$"Nemesis_Letter_VengeanceReturn{n}Title"`), so every numbered variant must exist in each language.
