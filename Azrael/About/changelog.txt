1.4.0
[h1]Azrael — 1.4.0[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Mountain start for The Deep Homestead[/b] - the default and Random starting site favor large hills or mountains. You can still pick any tile, and the part can be removed in the scenario editor. EN/CN/RU.
[*] [b]Safe removal summary[/b] - the removal report opens with one line: how many things to deconstruct, consume or sell, need attention, and are safe.
[*] [b]Multiplayer notice[/b] - with the Multiplayer mod active, Conflicts warns that safe-removal deconstruct only acts on your client.
[*] [b]New series mods show up on their own[/b] - any other active AzraelGodKing mod is listed in the hub, with its own Prepare to remove button.
[*] [b]Series events under the Azrael storyteller[/b] (AZR-343) - Living World refugees and warbands, Homesteader's Kats event, Stormproof weather, and Strata discoveries are picked more often when an event of their kind fires. How often events fire is unchanged, and big threats stay close to Cassandra. Other storytellers are unaffected.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Living World ↔ Deep Colony goodwill[/b] - the hub only says live when Deep Colony's goodwill listener actually registered with Living World.
[*] [b]Hub scrolling[/b] - the settings page no longer cuts off the last Prepare to remove buttons when the failed-patches section is shown.
[*] [b]Safe removal off-map pawns[/b] - Deep Colony perks and trauma on kidnapped colonists, quest guests, and other off-map pawns now count in the report.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Safe removal deconstruct[/b] - designations are queued and applied on the next game tick instead of straight from Mod Options. If the game is paused, they appear when you unpause.
[*] [b]Build stamps[/b] - the hub reads each series mod's BuildStamp when the mod exposes one, before falling back to the log.
[/list]
