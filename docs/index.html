Storage

Every piece protects its contents from deterioration — even outdoors.

Storage crate

3 stacks · 35 wood

A sturdy lidded crate that holds anything. The workhorse of a tidy stockyard.

Storage barrel

3 stacks · food & drink · 30 wood

Keeps food and drink out of the rain. It won't slow spoilage — preserve perishables first.

Pallet

2 stacks · 15 wood · walkable

Dirt cheap ground storage for anything — including stone chunks. Line your whole yard with them.

Food Preservation

No research or power required — perfect for tribal starts and the first winter.

Drying rack

no power · no research · 60 wood

Air-dry raw meat into jerky or fruit and vegetables into dried produce. Both keep for 60 days, weigh almost nothing, and make ideal caravan rations.

Smokehouse

wood-fired · 90 wood + 20 steel

Cure raw meat into smoked meat that keeps for a month with no freezer — and still cooks into meals like raw meat.

Jerky

keeps 60 days · ultralight

Tough, chewy, and it beats starving in a snowstorm.

Dried produce

Sun-dried harvest for the lean seasons.

Smoked meat

keeps 30 days · cooks into meals

A month of freezer-free meat for your cooks.

Installation

  1. Download the mod using the button above (or clone the repository).
  2. Copy the Homesteader folder into your RimWorld Mods directory:
    Windows: C:\Program Files (x86)\Steam\steamapps\common\RimWorld\Mods
    Linux: ~/.steam/steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/Mods
    macOS: ~/Library/Application Support/Steam/steamapps/common/RimWorld/Mods
  3. Enable Homesteader in the in-game mod list and restart.
Save compatibility: safe to add to an existing save. Before removing it, deconstruct the mod's buildings and use up or sell its items.
