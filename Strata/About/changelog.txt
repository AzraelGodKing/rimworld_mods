3.8.2
[h1]Strata - 3.8.2[/h1]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Cavern oxygen collapse on Medium/High[/b] (AZR-355) - breathing, plants, and animal methane now scale with room size. A huge Biomes! cavern full of wildlife no longer drains the whole layer to about 2% O2. Low only looked healthy before because it skipped animal breathing on the viewed map.
[*] [b]Low-oxygen rooms that never recovered[/b] (AZR-355) - fresh-air top-up no longer lowers its own target when a room is short on O2, so a depleted room refills to normal air.
[*] [b]Deep drill noise[/b] (AZR-272) - drilling out a portion now adds its noise burst on RimWorld 1.6.
[*] [b]Levels tab too small on first open[/b] (AZR-448) - the tab now sizes to your levels (at least three rows) the first time you open it, instead of opening as a thin strip you had to drag open.
[*] [b]Red and green glitch at full zoom-out[/b] (AZR-449) - open-sky gaps on upper levels keep showing the floor below at maximum zoom instead of flickering red and green.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Unused helpers[/b] — removed unused gravship, work-relay, portal, and shaft utility methods that had no callers.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Seismograph station[/b] (AZR-271) - the readout lists tremor, cave-in, and infestation risk for every linked underground level, not just its own floor. It refreshes about every 4 seconds.
[/list]
