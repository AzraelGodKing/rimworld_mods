[h1]Date Night — 1.1.1[/h1]

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Anniversaries[/b] — letter on the love-bond anniversary. A date that day is a special mood; letting it pass without one stings at midnight.
[*] [b]Favourite spots[/b] — couples remember where their best dates happened and prefer to go back. A ruined date can sour that venue.
[*] [b]Double dates[/b] — two couples with overlapping Date hours may share a table, picnic, or gather spot. Quality rolls once for the group; the evening spills into opinions of the other pair. If the other couple never shows, they date alone.
[*] [b]Update letter[/b] — loading a colony shows this version's notes from About/changelog.txt, with a link to the newest full notes.
[/list]

[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Harmony[/b] — date and private-time job drivers are no longer treated as patch classes (vanilla JobDriver.Cleanup). Dates apply instead of logging two red errors at load.
[/list]
