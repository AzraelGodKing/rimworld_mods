2.0.0
[h1]Homesteader — 2.0.0[/h1]
Homesteader 2 ships for both RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Your 1.6 colony keeps getting every update, and the same save carries over to 1.7.

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Well-stocked larder[/b] — colonists feel good when their cellars and pantries hold 3, 6 or 9 kinds of preserves.
[*] [b]Rain-aware water[/b] — rain barrels and cisterns fill faster in the rain and stop during a drought.
[*] [b]Bees need flowers[/b] — beehives only work with flowering crops, herbs or flowers nearby in the growing season.
[*] [b]Harvest festival[/b] — each colony's festival counts its own pantry, pumpkin pie or cider makes it a feast, a trader caravan comes to visit, and the letter names the preserves on the table.
[*] [b]Pantry tab[/b] — landrace bonuses show in the stock list, and your sort order is remembered.
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b] — Homesteader's buttons are synced when the Multiplayer mod is on, and gameplay settings travel with the save. Not tested yet.
[*] [b]Effects[/b] — a heat shimmer during the Kats heat dome and moving water in stone fountains that have water. Selecting a root cellar, icehouse or springhouse outlines every cooled cell. Both can be turned off in the settings.
[*] [b]Living World[/b] — famine rumours and war refugees get a short note about your own pantry.
[*] [b]Compatibility list[/b] — COMPATIBILITY.md lists every mod Homesteader works with and the names other mods rely on. Homesteader now marks the old standalone Wellspring as incompatible, since its buildings are part of Homesteader.
[*] [b]Settings per save[/b] — spoilage triage, allergy flares, favourite-food mood, egg rate, the Kats Effect and the new larder, water and bee options are saved with each colony. The settings window scrolls. In a game, the settings window changes that colony; at the main menu it sets the defaults for new games. Old saves start with your current settings.
[/list]

[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Tastes[/b] — only colonists, prisoners, slaves and guests you host get favourite foods and allergies. Raiders and traders no longer fill your save with tastes; old saves are cleaned up once on load. The Tastes tab shows "Not known yet" for anyone without tastes.
[*] [b]Kats Effect[/b] — no longer breaks if another mod's race or xenotype stops Kats from appearing.
[*] [b]RimWorld 1.7[/b] — the 1.7 folder now has its own .NET 10 build made against the 1.7 game files. 1.6 keeps its own build from the same source, and both load the same defs.
[*] [b]Refresh texture pack[/b] — the optional art pack is applied when the game starts, never mid-game. Turning it on or off takes effect on the next start.
[/list]
