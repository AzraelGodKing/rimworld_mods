HOMESTEADER

Tribal survival, farm-to-table crafting, brewing, beekeeping, weatherproof storage, homestead comforts, and off-grid power. Fully XML — no C# required.

STORMPROOF

Defend your colony's power grid from solar flares, lightning, short circuits, EMP, and toxic fallout — then summon the storm yourself. Custom C# behaviors with Harmony.

13 buildings · 4 research projects · 1 custom event · RimWorld 1.6

WELLSPRING

Dig wells, catch the rain, stockpile cisterns, and trench water into your fields for rich irrigated soil. Fully XML — no C# required.

8 buildings + irrigation · 3 research projects · RimWorld 1.4 / 1.5 / 1.6

STRATA

Dig down and build a true multistory base. Colonists commute between underground levels on their own to work, eat, and sleep — while heat rises, ore beckons, and the dark bites back. Custom C# with Harmony.

Installation

  1. Download the mod(s) using the buttons above.
  2. Extract the zip — it contains a single Homesteader, Stormproof, Wellspring, or Strata folder — into your RimWorld Mods folder.
  3. Enable the mod(s) in the in-game mod list and restart.
  4. Stormproof and Strata require the Harmony mod.
Save compatibility: safe to add mid-save. Before removing a mod, deconstruct its buildings and use up its items first.
