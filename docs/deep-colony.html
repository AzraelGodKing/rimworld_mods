Overview

Five interlocking systems that give each colonist a real identity — and make your colony feel like it has a living history. Not more furniture. Not another raid timer.

Needs: Harmony. Load Deep Colony after it. Inspect panels show perk points, mentor / apprentices, and active trauma.

What’s new in 2.0

The same five systems, deepened — and made readable. Three new colony tabs, full mod settings, and a heavier power tier you opt into.

Three colony tabs

Perks · Legacy · Reputation

See every colonist’s perks and unspent points, trace founder bloodlines and teaching lineages, and read the full goodwill ledger per faction.

Mod settings & presets

Soft / Default / Hard

Every system has its own toggle, plus sliders for combat shock, mentor XP, drift pacing, inheritance, and therapy strength.

Power tier (opt-in)

Hard preset

Skill-20 capstones, branching level-15 picks, respec, cross-skill archetypes, chronic trauma, and attitude-driven consequences.

Presets: Soft and Default keep power systems and attitude consequences off. Hard enables the heavier set — skill-20 capstones, branching perks, chronic trauma, and attitude-driven trade / caravan / raid pressure.

Perk trees

At skill levels 5 and 15, colonists earn perk points for permanent passive bonuses. Every skill has its own tree, with exact numbers in the tooltips.

Combat & medicine

Practiced eye → crack shot. Brawler’s instinct → battle hardened. First aid mastery → surgeon’s touch.

Craft & colony

Construction, Crafting, Plants, Animals, Mining, Intellectual, Social, Cooking, and Artistic — each with its own path.

Muscle memory

New in 2.0

Perks slow skill rust — and reclaiming lost levels earns double XP. Recruits who join past a gate arrive with points (or a modest pre-pick).

Capstones & branches

Hard preset

Skill 20 unlocks a capstone perk; level 15 becomes a branching pick. Respec at the cost of a cooldown and a bruised ego.

Cross-skill archetypes

Hard preset

Colonists whose perks span the right skills grow into archetypes — a named identity with its own bonus, earned from how they actually lived.

The Perks tab shows every colonist’s trees at a glance, and an alert nudges you when points sit unspent.

Trauma & therapy

Hostile downs, violent loss, captivity, mass-casualty raids, and stacked bereavement leave lasting psychological trauma that fades over weeks.

Trauma types

Combat shock, violent loss, captivity trauma, massacre survivor, and bereavement shock when grief stacks.

Specialty trauma

New in 2.0

Fire, toxic exposure, insect swarms, and betrayal each leave their own mark — and their own triggers to avoid.

Counsel

Doctors can counsel the wounded mind (workgiver or right-click). Therapy quality scales with the counselor’s Social skill, opinion, and the room. Casual therapy chats still help as a light supplement.

Group counseling

New in 2.0

One counselor, several patients — slower per pawn, but it keeps a shaken colony moving after a bad raid.

Scars & resilience

New in 2.0

Recovery leaves something behind: sometimes a lingering scar, sometimes resilience — a colonist genuinely hardened by what they survived.

Living with it

New in 2.0

Contextual flashbacks, combat habits shaped by old wounds, grudges against the faction responsible, and days of remembrance for the dead. Three counsel sessions forge a confidant bond that makes future therapy stronger.

Hard preset: trauma left untreated until it expires can settle into chronic stress — counseling eases it back down.

Apprenticeship

Pair a veteran with a rookie via right-click — pick the skill to focus on. Mentors must lead by 3+ skill levels; family only needs 2.

Passive learning

Apprentices earn bonus XP while working near their mentor.

Mentoring work

Dedicated Mentoring work type runs active teaching sessions. Multiple pairs can run at once — and a Biotech blackboard in the room boosts the XP.

Elders

New in 2.0

Colonists past 60 slow down at labor but shine as mentors, and earn one late perk point. Your founders stay valuable to the end.

Graduation & rivalry

New in 2.0

Graduating apprentices can pick up their mentor’s first perk — and maybe a new passion. Professional rivals in the same craft push each other’s XP.

Generational colonies

Children and recruits with colonist parents can inherit traits and passions. Build long enough and you’ll see echoes of your founders.

Deeper inheritance

New in 2.0

Dead parents and grandparents count too, families develop skill traditions, and adoptive caregivers can echo a passion onto their wards.

Founder surnames

New in 2.0

Colony-born children carry founder and parent surnames, so your bloodlines read like bloodlines.

Heirlooms

New in 2.0

When a colonist dies, a treasured possession passes to their kin — a small comfort with a long memory.

The Legacy tab traces founder lines, family traditions, and who taught whom.

Living faction reputation

Raids, trades, gifts, and shared-enemy kills nudge goodwill. Strong friendships and deep enmities slowly drift over time without constant contact — allies don’t collapse overnight from idle drift.

Reputation ledger

New in 2.0

The Reputation tab shows every faction’s ledger: what moved goodwill, when, and what drift is still pending.

Personal envoys

New in 2.0

Assign a colonist as envoy to a faction for steady goodwill — but if they die, the relationship takes it personally.

Settlement epithets

New in 2.0

Settlements earn epithets from your shared history — grudges and favors written on the world map.

Hard preset: attitude has teeth — trade prices, caravan access, and raid pressure follow how factions actually feel about you.
