One home, many floors — dig down and build a true multistory base

RimWorld 1.6 Requires Harmony C# mod Experimental
⬇ Download Strata View on GitHub

Overview

Build an excavated stairwell and a new underground level opens beneath your base: a solid stratum of mineable rock under a thick roof, ready to be carved into bedrooms, workshops, and freezers. Build another stairwell down there and keep going deeper.

Dependencies: requires the Harmony mod. Load Strata after Harmony in your mod list. Experimental: back up saves you care about.

The fluidity engine

What makes Strata different from older multi-level mods: instead of treating each floor as an island, colonists relay themselves between levels on their own. You designate, they commute.

RelayBehavior
WorkIdle colonists notice mining and plant designations, blueprints, hauling, and bills on other levels and commute down (or up) the stairs to do the work
FoodHungry pawns go find a meal on another level instead of starving next to a staircase
RestSleepy pawns walk home to their own bed — or to any level with a free bed to claim
HaulItems with no storage on their level get carried through the stairwell to a level whose stockpiles accept them

Buildings

Four ways down, up, and out of trouble.

Excavated stairwell

2×2 · 60 wood + 30 steel · Deep excavation research

A broad staircase carved down into the rock. Completing it opens a new underground level: solid stone to mine into rooms, workshops, and vaults, protected by a thick rock roof — safe from drop pods, mortars, and weather. Colonists use it on their own to work, eat, and sleep across levels. Seal it to wall a gas pocket or infestation onto one level; it cannot be deconstructed while anyone is still below.

Elevator

1×1 · 200W · Elevators research

Compact powered vertical transport that opens a level below, just like a stairwell. The car needs power to descend, but anyone below can always ride back up if the power fails — no one is ever stranded. Automatically ties the two levels' power grids together.

Shaft power conduit

1×1 · 10W · Shaft power research

Build one beside a stairwell on each level and wire each into its local grid: the pair ties the two levels' power nets together, sharing surplus to whichever level runs short, up to a cap. Keep a battery on each level for it to pool across.

Exhaust fan

1×1 · 100W · Electricity research

A powered extractor that pulls combustion fumes out of an enclosed room. Essential for running wood or chemfuel generators indoors or underground, where there's no open sky to carry the smoke away.

Life underground

The deeper you dig, the stranger it gets.

⇅

Heat rises, cold falls

Stairwells exchange temperature between the rooms at their top and bottom. A warm level below convects heat upward quickly; a warmer level above only bleeds down slowly. Put your freezer downstairs — generator heat drifts up, not in.

▲

Events know which way is up

Underground levels are sealed rock: solar flares, eclipses, toxic fallout, weather, and drop-pod raids are all suppressed down there. But hiding doesn't end a fight — raiders with nobody left to shoot at will pursue your colonists through the stairwells, unless you seal the hatch.

🐛

Threats of the deep

Infestations are the signature danger below, alongside cave-ins, breached gas pockets, and rare burrowing raiders who tunnel up through the stone. On the surface, ground tremors rattle your structures.

⛏

Riches of the deep

Levels sit on a geothermal gradient — deeper is warmer — and deeper levels hide richer ore. Miners can break into a lucky deep vein, and a wandering prospector may mark a hidden seam on the surface.

☁

Burners breathe

Wood-fired and chemfuel generators, campfires, torches, and open fires give off combustion fumes that pool in enclosed rooms as a visible black smog. Run one in a sealed room — or anywhere underground — and colonists start choking on a worsening smoke inhalation hediff that turns fatal if ignored. Vent it with an open roof, a door to the outdoors, or a powered exhaust fan. Solar, wind, geothermal, and batteries burn nothing and stay clean. Toggle “show smoke levels” in the play settings to read the exact smoke percentage under your cursor.

How it works

Each level is a real RimWorld map linked by stairwell pairs, built on the vanilla 1.6 pocket-map/portal system.

🗺

Real maps, not fakes

Pathfinding, temperature, rooms, and combat all work per-level exactly like vanilla. Every underground level is a genuine map with its own simulation.

🧭

Move the pawn, not the job

The AI patches move the colonist to the level where they're needed rather than building fragile cross-map jobs — vanilla AI takes over the moment they arrive, so every failure mode degrades safely.

💤

Framerate-friendly depth

Empty levels quietly throttle their background simulation, so a tall base doesn't tank your framerate.

📦

Logistics by stockpile

To move goods between levels, keep a stockpile near the stairs on each floor — haulers carry items up and down through the stairwell on their own.

Tips

  1. Thick rock roofs collapse when unsupported — leave pillars in big halls, just like mountain bases.
  2. Stairwells cannot be deconstructed while colonists are still below.
  3. Raiders who run out of targets on their level will find your stairwells and come down (or up) after you. A sealed stairwell stops them cold — but it stops your colonists too.

Installation

  1. Install the Harmony mod first.
  2. Download with the button above and extract the Strata folder into your RimWorld Mods directory.
  3. Enable Harmony then Strata in the mod list. Load Strata after Harmony.
  4. Restart the game.
Save compatibility: safe to add mid-save. Before removing the mod: bring everyone to the surface, haul up anything you care about, and deconstruct all stairwells.
