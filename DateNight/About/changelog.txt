2.0.0
[h1]Date Night — 2.0.0[/h1]
Date Night 2 ships for both RimWorld 1.6 and 1.7. Your 1.6 colony keeps getting every update, and the same save carries over to 1.7.

[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Ask out tonight[/b] — select a colonist with a partner and press Ask out tonight. They go on one date at the next free evening hour, with no schedule painting.
[*] [b]Romance panel[/b] — the Romance button shows the couple's stage, time together, anniversary, last date, favourite spot and double-date rapport.
[*] [b]Gift picker[/b] — add gifts from a searchable list. Names that no longer match an item show in red instead of being ignored silently.
[*] [b]Smarter dates[/b] — how much the two like each other, Kind or Abrasive partners, a fine meal and an impressive room now shape how a date goes. Couples lean toward what they enjoy: gourmands toward dinner, ascetics toward walks and stars, night owls toward stargazing, artists toward picnics.
[*] [b]Both partners give gifts[/b] — the giver alternates by day.
[*] [b]Fishing dates[/b] (Odyssey) — by one of your fishing zones.
[*] [b]Trying for a baby[/b] (Biotech) — a toggle on a lover pair who can conceive. Lovin comes more often until she is pregnant. Pregnant partners skip dancing and long walks.
[*] [b]Proposals[/b] — a run of wonderful dates makes a proposal more likely.
[*] [b]Multiplayer[/b] — Date Night's buttons are synced when the Multiplayer mod is on. All players need the host's Date Night settings. Not tested yet.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Settings[/b] are grouped into sections and scroll, so long translations fit on small screens.
[*] [b]Nobles[/b] only eat meals their title allows on a dinner date.
[*] [b]Ideology[/b] — an ideoligion that disapproves of lovin gives no good mood for Lovin hours.
[*] [b]Homesteader[/b] — the harvest maypole is the first choice for dance dates. [b]Niceties[/b] — the Lovin bed claim leaves rooms Niceties shares alone.
[*] [b]Schedule tab Date button[/b] no longer moves one cell right for every mod that touches the schedule grid. If a mod Date Night does not know about adds a button, use the new column offset setting.
[*] [b]RimWorld 1.7[/b] — the 1.7 folder now has its own .NET 10 build made against the 1.7 game files. 1.6 keeps its own build from the same source.
[*] [b]RimJobWorld[/b] — the RJW checks are bound once at startup instead of called through reflection every second, so scheduled Lovin costs less with RJW on.
[*] [b]Performance[/b] — the once-a-second schedule check skips colonists who are on neither slot, partners and colonists are looked up once per tick, the Lovin bed is remembered instead of searched for, and starting a date scans the map once instead of three or more times. Bigger colonies and older saves gain the most.
[/list]
[b]Fixed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Boyfriend/girlfriend stuck[/b] — couples on the gradual-romance ladder were treated as broken up after every date, so boyfriend/girlfriend dropped back to sweethearts and rarely became lovers. Only real lovers can break up now.
[*] [b]Dates started from free time[/b] now start the friendship and can become a double date, the same as dates started by the schedule.
[*] [b]Schedule mismatch alert[/b] only names real couples (lovers or pairs already on the ladder), not colonists who might become friends.
[*] [b]Dinner dates[/b] count toward the colonist's nutrition-eaten record.
[*] [b]Saves[/b] now keep an open Date or Lovin window, so made-it and stood-up moods still land when you load in the middle of one.
[/list]

1.8.0
[h1]Date Night — 1.8.0[/h1]
[b]Added[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]Gradual romance.[/b] Adults without a lover can date as friends, then sweethearts, then boyfriend/girlfriend. A few good dates and enough opinion become a vanilla lover pair. Lovin stays locked until they are lovers or spouses. Existing couples skip the ladder.
[*] [b]Romance on the Rim soft-compat.[/b] Date Night yields while RotR is already running a kiss, stroll, proposal, or other romance job. A finished date fills RotR's romance need (stroll / lovin / kiss scale; more on a wonderful or anniversary date). No hard dependency. Friends without that need are a no-op.
[/list]
[b]Changed[/b]
[list]
[*] [b]1.6 / 1.7 dual package confirmed.[/b] Official 1.6 loads 1.6/Assemblies. Unstable 1.7 loads 1.7/Assemblies. New romance state is save-safe on 1.6.1+ colonies.
[*] [b]Faster pawn lookups[/b] on date/venue ticks (per-tick id cache).
[/list]
